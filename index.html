How the ranking works

The flip

Put in a buy order one tick (0.1) above the best buy order. When it fills, list a sell offer one tick below the cheapest sell offer. Margin = sell price × (1 − tax) − buy price. Tax is only charged when you sell: 1.25%, or 1.125% / 1% with Bazaar Flipper. Mayor perks that change tax (such as Derpy's QUAD TAXES) are detected from the live election API and applied automatically.

Volume

Your buy order fills when players instant-sell, and your sell offer fills when they instant-buy. We take the 7-day totals of both from the API, divide by 168 hours, and use the smaller side, because a flip is only as fast as its slower leg.

Profit / hour

Your coins can't be reused until both orders fill, so profit/hr = profit per flip ÷ (buy-order fill time + sell-offer fill time + ~6 min to claim and relist). Fill speed = the item's volume × your share. In Auto mode your share is 1 ÷ (competing orders near the top + 1), because you only get fills while you hold the top spot. Orders are sized to about 2h of fills and respect the 71,680-item and 1B-coin caps.

Score & confidence

Profit/hr is multiplied by a confidence factor built from liquidity, how believable the spread is (50%+ margins are penalized), competition, buy/sell balance, and 7-day history. The top items are checked against Coflnet's 7-day price data: Steady margin matches its normal margin, Above normal is a temporary gap that will likely close, and Unusual spread is an item that normally isn't flippable. Checked items get a ✓. The best flip right now scores 100.

Craft flips

Recipes come from the community NotEnoughUpdates repo, refreshed daily. Patient: buy orders on the ingredients, then a sell offer on the result. Instant: insta-buy everything, craft, insta-sell. That's rare but risk-free when it's positive. Crafts/hr is limited by both the result's buyers and every ingredient's sellers. Many recipes need a collection level, which is shown on each item.

Flags

Dead spread the ask is over 4× the bid. Illiquid fewer than 1 unit trades per hour. Price spike the ask is 30%+ above its recent median, a classic manipulation pattern. One-sided the price is trending. NPC floor an NPC buys it for about your buy price, so your downside is limited.

Planner

Greedy allocation: it repeatedly picks the flip with the best risk-adjusted profit for the coins left, caps each item at your “max per item” share to stay diversified, and sizes orders to about an hour of your share of volume. Each flip uses a buy and a sell order slot: 14 slots by default, and 21 or 28 with Bazaar Flipper.

Freshness

Prices load straight from api.hypixel.net in your browser. The status bar shows how old Hypixel's snapshot is, and warns if it's more than 3 minutes behind. A GitHub Action also saves an hourly snapshot into a rolling 4-day history for the charts and spike detection. No API key is needed.

Game limits to know

Max 71,680 items per buy order, 15B coins of new orders per day, 1B coins in sell offers at once, and orders expire after 7 days. Prices change constantly, so check the in-game order book before committing.
